Data Deletion

How to delete your data from SpinDex AI

You can delete your data from SpinDex AI at any time. Most of your data is stored locally on your device and can be removed directly in the app or by clearing the app’s data. If you have enabled cloud sync, follow the steps below to remove cloud data as well.

Option 1 — Delete tasks and memories in the app

  • Open SpinDex AI and delete any task, memory note, or attachment you want removed using the delete (trash) action.
  • Deleted tasks are removed from your list and, if cloud sync is enabled, from cloud storage.

Option 2 — Clear the app’s data on your device

  • Android: Settings → Apps → SpinDex AI → Storage → Clear data (or Clear storage).
  • iOS: Delete the app from your device. This removes all locally stored data.

Option 3 — Request full deletion

To request deletion of any remaining data (including your registered email and cloud-synced data), contact us:

Email: support@generalsoft.ai

Please include the email address associated with your account (if any) and the subject line “Delete my data”.

What happens when you delete

Once deletion is complete, your tasks, memories, attachments, and account/verification information are removed and cannot be recovered. Some anonymized or aggregated data that does not identify you may be retained for security or legal purposes.

We process deletion requests promptly, typically within 30 days, and will confirm once your data has been removed.
