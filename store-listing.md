### Full description (4,000 chars max)
```
SpinDex AI turns your to-do list into something you can almost touch.

Task management has always lived in flat, boring lists. SpinDex AI gives it a
physical, tactile feel — like flipping through a Rolodex, scanning a wall of
colorful tiles, or shuffling a 3D stack of sticky notes. Capture tasks by voice,
snap a photo of a receipt, and let a private AI assistant do the heavy lifting.

THREE TACTILE VIEWS
• Rolodex — cards flip like a physical Rolodex, with auto-advance you can pause
  and play.
• Icons — a tidy grid of colored tiles with one-tap actions.
• 3D Stack — sticky notes with realistic shadows and rotation.

AI VIRTUAL EMPLOYEE
Tap the sparkle on any task and SpinDex AI breaks it down, sets priorities, and
suggests next steps. It can act as your Executive Assistant, Research Analyst,
Project Manager, Creative Consultant, or Memory Scribe — powered by your own
local AI server (Ollama or custom), so your data stays under your control.

VOICE INPUT
Dictate tasks hands-free. Your words appear in real time and the mic turns red
while it listens.

PHOTOS, PDFS & RECEIPTS
Attach images and PDFs to any task. SpinDex AI auto-detects receipts so you can
expense them in a tap.

REMEMBER ANYTHING
Start a note with "remember…" and SpinDex AI stores it as a memory the AI can
recall later — without cluttering your task list.

PINS & CATEGORIES
Organize with Home, Work, and custom categories. Pin bookmarks and parked ideas
so they stay out of your daily view.

DUE DATES & REMINDERS
Set due dates, see approaching and overdue tasks flagged in red, and get
reminded before it's too late.

CLOUD SYNC
Sync your tasks across devices with a clear sync indicator and offline-safe
local storage.

Your tasks, in physical form. Download SpinDex AI today.
```

### Data safety (Google Play "Data safety" form)
Declare the following in Play Console based on the app's behavior:

- **Location** — Collected, not shared (optional, for meeting titles / task categories)
- **Personal info → Email address** — Collected, not shared (account verification)
- **Photos and videos** — Collected, not shared (task attachments)
- **Audio → Voice or sound recordings** — Collected, not shared (voice input)
- **Contacts** — Collected, not shared (people referenced in tasks)
- **Calendar** — Collected, not shared (meetings & reminders)
- **Device or other IDs** — Collected, not shared (installation UUID)
- **App info and performance → Diagnostics** — Collected, not shared (telemetry)

Data deletion: provide `https://spindex.generalsoft.ai/data-deletion.html` and enable the
"Users can request deletion" path (email `support@generalsoft.ai`).
